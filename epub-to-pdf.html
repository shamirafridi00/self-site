EPUB and MOBI to PDF, at print quality

Shelf lays your eBook out page by page with its own fonts and styles, then saves it through your browser’s PDF engine, so text stays sharp and selectable and images keep their full resolution.

Choose an EPUB or MOBI bookor drop it anywhere on this page. It never leaves your device.
How it works

How to convert an EPUB to PDF

  1. Choose your book

    EPUB 2, EPUB 3 and classic MOBI/PRC files are supported.

  2. Set up the pages

    Pick A4, Letter, A5 or 6 × 9 in, margins, text size, chapter breaks and a contents page.

  3. Check the preview

    See exactly how the book will look before you save.

  4. Save as PDF

    Press Save as PDF and choose “Save as PDF” as the destination in the dialog.

FAQ

Questions about EPUB & MOBI to PDF

Why does a print dialog open?

Shelf uses your browser’s built-in PDF engine, the same one behind “Print to PDF”. That’s what keeps text selectable and images at full quality. In the dialog, choose Save as PDF, leave margins on Default and turn on Background graphics.

Are images and the cover kept?

Yes. Images are embedded at their original resolution, and the cover can be added as the first page.

Can I convert DRM-protected books?

No. Books with DRM are locked to their store’s apps and can’t be opened by any third-party tool.

Does it support AZW3 or KF8 Kindle files?

Only classic MOBI files are supported. Convert AZW3 to EPUB first with a tool such as Calibre, then use that.

Can I choose the page size?

Yes: A4, US Letter, US Legal, A5 or a 6 × 9 in book size, with narrow, normal, wide or no margins.
