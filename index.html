FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Shelf really free?

Yes. Every tool is free, with no sign-up, no watermarks and no page limits. The only limit is your device’s memory, so very large files convert more slowly on older phones.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Shelf runs entirely in your browser. Your PDFs and eBooks are opened, converted and stored on your own device and are never sent anywhere. Only the app’s code and fonts load from public CDNs.

Why can’t Gemini or ChatGPT open my PDF?

Usually the file is encrypted or permission-restricted (common with bank and credit-card statements), uses an unusual internal encoding, or is a scan with no text layer. Fix PDF for AI rebuilds the pages into a fresh, unlocked PDF, and PDF to Markdown pulls out the exact text and tables, which AI tools read reliably.

How do I convert only some pages of a PDF to JPG?

Open PDF to JPG, choose your file, then type the pages you want, like 1-5, 8, 12-, or click the page thumbnails. Only the selected pages are converted, and they download together as one ZIP.

Can I convert a password-protected PDF?

Yes, if you know the password. Shelf asks for it when the file opens, and it is used only on your device. The converted file is a new copy without the password.

Which eBook formats can the reader open?

EPUB (2 and 3), PDF and classic MOBI/PRC. DRM-protected books and Kindle KF8/AZW3 files aren’t supported; convert those to EPUB first with a tool such as Calibre.

Does PDF to Markdown keep tables and images?

Yes. Rows and columns are rebuilt as Markdown tables, headings and lists keep their structure, and embedded images are saved in an images folder linked from the Markdown. Very complex layouts may need light tidying.

Where is my library stored?

In your browser’s private storage on this device. It stays between visits, but clearing site data or using a private window removes it, so keep your original files as a backup.
