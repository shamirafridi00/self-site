Good to know

What the error means

The uploaded file could not be opened or read. Please re-upload the document, share it as an image (such as a PNG or JPEG), or copy and paste the relevant text… likely due to file encryption, password protection, or an unsupported/corrupted PDF encoding.

Banks and card issuers often encrypt statements or restrict copying. Your PDF viewer opens them fine, but many AI upload pipelines can’t. Some PDFs also use custom font encodings that turn text into gibberish when extracted.

AI-ready PDF redraws every page into a new file with none of that baggage. Markdown extracts the real text and tables, which is the most accurate way to get figures like cheque numbers, payees and amounts into a chat.

FAQ

Questions about Fix PDF for AI

Why does Gemini or ChatGPT say my PDF can’t be opened?

The most common causes are encryption or permission restrictions (typical for bank statements), unusual font encodings, damaged file structure, or a file that’s too large. Your PDF viewer is more forgiving than AI upload systems, which is why it opens fine on your computer.

Which option should I choose?

Start with AI-ready PDF if you want to upload the whole document. If you need exact figures, such as amounts, dates and reference numbers, use the Markdown tab and paste the text. Use Images to send just a few pages.

Will the AI still read the numbers correctly?

At 150 dpi or higher the pages are sharp and AI models read them well. For long lists of figures, Markdown is more reliable because it gives the AI the actual text instead of a picture of it.

Is it safe to use with bank statements?

Yes. Everything happens in your browser and nothing is uploaded to Shelf. The only place your document goes is wherever you choose to send the result.

The new file is too big for the AI. What can I do?

Lower the resolution to 110 dpi, reduce quality, tick Black & white, or set “Split every N pages” to create several smaller PDFs.

Does it remove a password I don’t know?

No. If a PDF needs a password to open, you have to enter it. Shelf then creates a new copy that doesn’t need one.
