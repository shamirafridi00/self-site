How it works

How to convert PDF pages to JPG

  1. Choose your PDF

    Drop it on the page or press Choose PDF. If the file has a password, Shelf will ask for it.

  2. Pick the pages

    Type ranges like 1-5, 8, 12- (12 to the end), or click thumbnails. Shift-click selects a whole run.

  3. Set format and resolution

    JPG for small files, PNG for razor-sharp text, 150 dpi for screens and AI, 300 dpi for print.

  4. Save

    Get every image in one ZIP, or in Chrome and Edge write them straight into a folder on your computer.

FAQ

Questions about PDF to JPG & PNG

What resolution should I choose?

150 dpi is right for screens, slides and AI tools. Use 200 dpi when the document has small print, and 300 dpi when you plan to print the images.

Can it handle a PDF with hundreds of pages?

Yes. Pages are converted one at a time to keep memory low. For very large jobs in Chrome or Edge, choose “Write into a folder” so images go straight to disk instead of into one big ZIP.

How do I send the pages to ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude?

Convert just the pages you need, unzip the download and attach the images to your chat. If the AI accepts PDFs, the AI-ready PDF tool keeps everything in one file instead.

Why do my images arrive in a ZIP?

Browsers ask permission for every separate download, so several pages are bundled into one ZIP. A single page downloads as a plain image.

Is there a watermark or page limit?

No. Shelf adds nothing to your images and has no page limits.
